Clip-by-clip dubbing

Dub each clip while the original plays in your ears.

  1. Load your originals. Pick the mp3 files, or a single .zip of them.
  2. Record each cue. Put on wired headphones and tap the red button. The original plays while you dub, or you can listen first and dub after a beep. Recording runs until you tap the button again, so longer lines are fine.
  3. Compare, then export. Play your take, the original, or both at once. Redo any cue, then export every take as an mp3 with its original’s file name.
